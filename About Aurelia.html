Our Story

ABOUT AURELIA

Design Begins With Understanding.

Aurelia Interiors is a contemporary interior design studio based in Sydney, creating refined residential and commercial spaces across Australia. For over 7 years, we have created spaces shaped around the people who use them from private residences and apartments to workplaces, hospitality venues and retail environments. With 100+ projects completed across Australia, our work brings together thoughtful planning, considered materials and a strong understanding of how people live, work and experience a space.

Our MISSION

Creating Spaces That Feel Like They Belong. We design for how a space will actually be lived in, not just how it looks on day one. The goal is a space that still feels right in ten years, not one that peaks the day the photos are taken.

OUR APPROACH

We begin by understanding how a space is lived in, what our clients need from it and how they want it to feel. From there, we bring together spatial planning, materials, lighting, furniture and detail to create an interior that feels like it was always meant to be there.
