A Destination Designed to Linger

The brief was simple in principle but ambitious in execution: create a destination that could feel equally natural for a quiet afternoon meeting, an intimate dinner, or a late evening surrounded by music and conversation. Aurelia was brought in to shape the experience from the ground up, creating a seamless design language that could carry the atmosphere from the main lounge and private dining spaces to the rooftop restaurant and after-dark jazz lounge. Warm stone, walnut, brushed brass and soft neutral upholstery establish a sense of understated luxury, while layered lighting allows each space to evolve as the day moves into night. We paid particular attention to the moments guests might otherwise overlook, treating corridors, restrooms and private executive areas with the same level of care as the social spaces. The result is a sophisticated hospitality environment that feels intimate despite its scale, designed to welcome, linger, celebrate and return to.
